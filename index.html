Logistics & Elevation Calculation Logic

1. Road Freight & Vehicle Mileage Formula

Road logistics cost combines distance-based fleet rates, baseline fuel consumption, vehicle payload weight, and incline elevation penalties.

Total Fuel (Liters) = (Distance / Mileage) + Extra Incline Fuel
Fuel Cost (₹) = Total Fuel × Diesel Price (₹/L)
Total Road Cost (₹) = (Distance × Fleet Base Rate) + (Distance × Weight × Rate/Ton-Km) + Incline Fuel Cost

2. Eco-Incline Ascent Physics & Fuel Penalty Formula

Climbing steep inclines consumes extra fuel due to potential energy work ($W = m \cdot g \cdot h$) against gravity and thermal powertrain losses.

Gross Mass (kg) = (Tare Vehicle Weight 12T + Cargo Payload) × 1000
Potential Energy (MJ) = Gross Mass × 9.81 m/s² × Total Ascent (meters) / 10⁶
Extra Incline Diesel (Liters) = Potential Energy (MJ) / (38.6 MJ/L × 0.38 Engine Efficiency)
Incline Savings (₹) = (Shortest Route Liters - Eco Route Liters) × Diesel Rate

3. CMVR Post-2018 Empirical Fuel & Mileage Model (India MAV / Rigid)

Under India's Motor Vehicles Act / CMVR gross weight limits (post-2018 revision), 6-axle articulated trucks (C3S3 model up to ~49t-52t GVW) and 2/3-axle rigid trucks follow empirical fuel consumption equations $C$ (mL/km) converted to Indian standard practice Mileage (km/L):

Mileage (km/L) = 1000 / C (mL/km)

6-Axle MAV / Trailer (C3S3 Model, W = Gross Weight in Tons, S = Slope %, V = Speed km/h):
• Ghat/Hill (S: 2–7%, V: 25–35 km/h): C = -49.32W + 30.24V + 20.69(WS) + 0.355W² - 24.96S² - 0.515V² - 0.0218(W²S²)
• Plains/Highway (S < 2%, V: 40–60 km/h): C = 11.67W - 1.79V + 2.95(WS) + 0.0932(WV) - 0.136W²

2/3-Axle Rigid Truck (W = Gross Weight in Tons, S = Slope %, V = Speed km/h):
• Ghat/Hill (S: 2–7%, V: 25–45 km/h): C = -8.01W + 105.64S - 3.65V + 15.20(WS) - 20.51S² - 0.027(W²S²)
• Plains (S: 0.6–2%, V: 50–70 km/h): C = 14.60W + 38.80S + 2.48V - 0.208(WV) - 1.245(SV) + 0.135(WSV)

Slope Fuel Penalty %:
Extra Fuel % = [ ( C(S₂) - C(S₁) ) / C(S₁) ] × 100

5. Indian Railways Freight Formula (IRCTC / CONCOR Tariff)

Rail freight charges are computed siding-to-siding based on weight thresholds and distance class tiers.

Chargeable Weight (Tons) = Max(Payload Tons, 10 Tons Threshold)
Base Linehaul = Distance × Chargeable Weight × ₹1.45 / Ton-Km
If Payload > 25 Tons ➔ Apply 12% Bulk Wagon Discount: Linehaul × 0.88
Total Rail Cost (₹) = ₹4,500 Siding & Drayage Fee + Linehaul

6. Air Freight Volumetric & Chargeable Weight Rating

IATA standard dimensional weight formula determines whether cargo rate is charged on actual weight or volumetric size.

Cargo Volume (m³) = Length (m) × Width (m) × Height (m)
Air Volumetric Weight (kg) = Volume (m³) × 167 kg/m³
Air Chargeable Weight (kg) = Max(Actual Cargo Weight, Volumetric Weight)
Total Air Cargo Cost (₹) = ₹3,500 Terminal Fee + (Distance × Chargeable Weight × ₹0.048 / kg / km)
